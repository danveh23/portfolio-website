Common Questions

How do the 3 free analyses work? ▾
Every visitor gets 3 completely free analyses — no registration, no credit card needed. After that, you'll need to choose a package to continue. The counter is stored in your browser and never resets.
How do I pay after my free analyses? ▾
Contact us via the contact page or email us at guardifyapp2025@gmail.com to choose your package. We'll set you up and provide access within 24 hours.
Can I analyze any website, including my competitor's? ▾
Yes — Guardify can analyze any publicly accessible website URL. This is great for benchmarking your site against competitors and seeing where you stand.
Is the Basic package good for beginners? ▾
Absolutely. The Basic package is designed to be simple and understandable — no technical knowledge needed. You'll get clear, actionable tips you can implement right away.
What does the PDF report include? ▾
The PDF report (Standard and Premium) contains all findings, scores, prioritized issues with severity levels, step-by-step fix instructions, and performance metrics — professionally formatted and ready to share with your team or client.
What is the email consultation in the Premium plan? ▾
After your analysis, our team will review the results and send you a short personalized email with expert advice tailored to your specific situation — going beyond the automated report.
